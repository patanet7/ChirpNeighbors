# ChirpNeighbors — Spring Garden source candidates

Generated 17 October 2026. Three new concepts: standing blue jay, flowering shrub, and cedar platform feeder. These are UNAPPROVED SOURCE CANDIDATES, not production sprites, animation frames, or admitted runtime assets.

Open preview.html for the original pixels on light and dark review surfaces. All five original generation outputs are preserved unchanged in source/. The second bird and shrub versions are a targeted edge-cleanup attempt, not an accepted fix.

## Visual review
- Blue jay: strong recognizable crest, black collar, white face and blue barred wing/tail; crisp feather structure. The crest is dramatic and the wing pattern is stylized. Feet, proportions and markings still need biological review. Raw-image viewing shows blue/white haze, but it is not apparent in the light/dark composited preview. Nonbinary alpha remains.
- Flowering shrub: coherent spring foliage and blossoms, attractive arching branches. The second pass is crisper and more saturated. Raw-image viewing shows green/cream haze, but it is not apparent in the light/dark composited preview, including branch gaps. Nonbinary alpha remains. The treatment is detailed pixel-art-adjacent rather than a strict low-color sprite.
- Cedar feeder: restrained side/front elevation and plausible construction; wide low silhouette. No broad glow is apparent, but thin edge fringe and soft alpha remain. Seeds may be too large at scene scale.

## Provenance and admission boundary
The supplied reference bundle was materialized and its robin, yard and object boards visually inspected. Its source contracts establish a 960 × 540 side-view world, separate source density/world size, and unapproved concept status. No reference image pixels were passed as generation input; prompts were based on visual inspection. Native image generation was used, followed by one targeted image-generation edit for bird and shrub. Raw-view haze initially suggested a failure, but actual light/dark composites substantially resolve that concern; alpha remains nonbinary and unvalidated. Model name/version and random seed were not exposed by the tool and are not invented. Prompts, original output filenames, byte hashes and alpha measurements are retained.

All outputs contain partial alpha. Raw RGB has colored haze outside subjects; composited preview does not show broad haze. Alpha presence is NOT alpha correctness or validation. No palette reduction, resampling, masking, background removal, sprite conversion, or production integration was performed. Current runtime limits include 256-pixel maximum source edge, 64 frames, 65,536 decoded pixels and 64 palette colors with binary alpha; these large source candidates do not meet those requirements. No animations are included. Next step is visual/species review, followed by an authorized deterministic pixel/alpha cleanup and compatible adapter workflow.

All three were visually inspected in the PDF at proposed silhouette widths of 72 / 180 / 150 world pixels.
