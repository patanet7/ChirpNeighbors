# San Diego plant candidates batch 02
Eight immutable generated originals; five candidate source images across Cleveland sage, deergrass and chalk dudleya; three grass attempts rejected/held and excluded from runtime derivatives. This expands the local collection to six species across batches 01–02, not the full regional roster.

Cleveland sage (Salvia clevelandii): flowering and spent-flower whole shrubs, not separate foliage/wood layers. Widely spaced violet flower whorls change to dry calyx/seed whorls while gray-green foliage persists. The generator exposed roots in both states: these need removal or explicit ground occlusion before placement. It also regenerated roots and leaf pixels, so root invariance is not validated; alpha128 overlap is 0.87525.

Deergrass (Muhlenbergia rigens): usable-for-review vegetative fountain-shaped fine-leaf mound. Initial generation incorrectly made pampas-like plumes; an already-running stress variation inherited the same error. A narrow-head correction was still insufficiently convincing. All three are preserved under rejected/ and excluded from production counts and derivatives. A final image-generation edit removed flower stems entirely to give the current vegetative candidate. No correct flowering or stress state is claimed.

Chalk dudleya (Dudleya pulverulenta): unchanged silvery chalky rosette plus separate flowering stalk overlay gives invariant basal plant placement. Pendant red flowers are appropriate in concept, but the drawing is smoother than the desired pixel style, stalks are enlarged for readability, and the overlay base sits over front leaves. Attachment/scale and species distinction from similar cultivated succulents need review. Summer dormancy is missing, not represented as orange autumn or snow.

All source images are 1254-square; 512-square derivatives are uniform nearest-neighbor whole-canvas resizes, not new art. No source palette reduction, alpha thresholding, crop, retouch or scripted plant generation occurred. Exact prompts, SHA-256, source dimensions, alpha bounds, proposed root/attachment anchors, world-size assumptions and registration metrics are retained. Wind and health simulation are not implemented. Regions are proposals pending authored masks and engine tests.
