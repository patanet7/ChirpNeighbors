# ChirpNeighbors corvid candidates
North American starter assumptions: American Crow (Corvus brachyrhynchos) and Common Raven (Corvus corax). Six built-in image_gen calls: two canonicals, four action sheets. Generated from the project's blue-jay style reference and subsequent generated canonicals. No external photograph was passed to image_gen or redistributed in this bundle.

## Biological source canon
- Cornell American Crow identification: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/American_Crow/id — length 40–53 cm, black plumage, moderate bill. Accessed 2026-10-17.
- Cornell Common Raven identification: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Common_Raven/id — length 56–69 cm; heavier bill, shaggy throat, wedge tail; strutting and occasional two-foot hops. Accessed 2026-10-17.
- Cornell comparison: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/news/similar-species-crows-and-ravens/ — crow tail rounded/square vs raven tapering/diamond; raven larger. Accessed 2026-10-17.
These are factual references, not licenses to reuse Cornell/Macaulay photographs or recordings. None are included.

## Candidate QA, not final approval
Crow and raven are distinct; raven bill and throat shag are strongly emphasized. Crow remains too shaggy and both have slightly illustrative eyes. Raven's curved bill may be overemphasized. Crow walk contains true leg changes but weak near/far alternation; pairs of frames are similar. Raven hop has actual crouch, push, airborne, landing changes, but wing action is exaggerated and posture/scale consistency needs refinement. Both idle sheets have actual eyelid closure and head rotation. Tail and feather shapes drift across generated poses. No synthetic bobbing or frame morphing added.
The raw image viewer exposes dark hidden RGB outside alpha. Cream-composited previews verify those dark backdrops are transparent, not solid backgrounds. Some soft edge alpha remains; originals preserved unchanged. Source cell partition adjusted for crow idle to avoid neighboring tail contamination. Runtime uses one fixed 0.48 scale for every action cell, without per-frame auto-fit. Frames are 384x384, atlases 768x768. Nominal ground pivots are cell-space provisional anchors, not manually stabilized contact points. Therefore foot-position jitter remains visible and requires review before integration.
Physical size is metadata separate from canvases: suggested length midpoint ratio raven/crow 62.5/46.5 = 1.344. This is a rendering convention from ranges, not an individual measured biological ratio.
