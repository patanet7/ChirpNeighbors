# Material correction lessons

- A full-sheet style reference dominated by concrete can transfer the wrong material into a requested ceramic object. For a material correction, use the failed isolated object for form and explicitly replace its surface cues.
- Positive optical cues matter: intact glaze, coherent edge/top highlights and a calm darker reflection plane made the material legible. A negative instruction to avoid speckles alone would not prove ceramic.
- Keep controls bounded. This pass changed reference scope and the material prompt, made one edit call, preserved its original, and performed a scoped comparison. No repeated identical prompting or hidden retry occurred.
- Register at the object body and placement anchor. A generator may change output resolution; equal image canvases alone do not preserve world scale. The revised sprite matches the prior424px body height in its292×457 canvas while retaining source-resolution bytes.
- Compare raw RGB with actual alpha composites. Saturated RGB at alpha1–6 is not evidence of a visible neon edge. Test light and dark backgrounds before reporting a halo; preserve original pixels and record the threshold diagnostic rather than silently color-keying.
- Surface contrast is supporting description, not a material classifier. Smooth plastic could also have low grain. The ceramic-like reading comes from the visual glaze/form combination; owner/style/runtime acceptance remains separate from this material correction gate.
- A material-review pass creates one revised variant with multiple derivatives. It does not create another canonical family, an animation, independent layers, or final acceptance.
