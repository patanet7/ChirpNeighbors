# San Diego starter plant compatibility catalog

The editable JSON and CSV catalog 29 provisional whole-plant variants across 25 canonical families. 19 families are regional outdoor or cultivated patio candidates; 6 are indoor/sheltered-interior nursery stock. Four of the 29 whole-plant entries are alternate dry/spent states of existing families. Nothing is newly generated or owner accepted.

Use `plant-compatibility-catalog.json` as the complete compatibility view, `plant-compatibility-catalog.csv` for editing/comparison, and `compatibility-groups.json` for scene option lists. Each record preserves the canonical asset/variant pair, source manifest/hash/pointer, sprite hash/dimensions, provisional anchor, footprint, scale, z-order, mounting surface, seasonal/state needs and wind metadata. Relative paths support relocation under an intake yard-production root. No production index or original manifest is changed. Edits to this audit are proposed compatibility changes, not automatic canonical edits.

The 52 audited production entries also include tree components, detached leaves/needles/cones, ground details and held entries. Those are classified separately. Seven production manifests and three later review/evidence sources are snapshotted byte-for-byte. All 52 sprite hashes and declared dimensions match, and all have real transparent alpha. Source snapshots include original prompts/rights and review history; sprite pixels remain in the existing production packages and are not duplicated in this audit ZIP.

## Scene options

| Scene | Primary palette | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Coastal garden | Torrey pine, California bush sunflower, San Diego monkeyflower, Shaw's agave | Torrey pine and Shaw's agave have restricted native contexts; these are regional planted options. Other sunflower species remain distinct IDs. |
| Shaded oak yard | Bright-edge candidates: hollyleaf cherry, blue-eyed grass, common yarrow | No independent usable oak tree is available from these audited local sources. A baked oak in a yard background remains baked. Do not treat these as a certified deep-shade plant list. |
| Mediterranean courtyard | Cultivated jade and trailing ivy geranium | Patio pots are nonnative cultivated options. Ivy geranium belongs on an elevated ledge/table; its pot and trailing foliage are one sprite. Regional bed/rosette options are listed separately. |
| Existing inland | Sugar bush, white sage, black sage, showy penstemon | Sycamore is an optional large moist/riparian or irrigated-pocket specimen, not a default dry hillside tree. |
| Existing patio | Jade and ivy geranium | Monstera, pothos, snake plant, ZZ plant, spider plant and rubber plant remain sheltered/interior-only options; they are not automatically enabled outdoors. |

These groups are scene-composition proposals grounded in existing manifest contexts, not new botanical validation, a guarantee of horticultural coexistence, or owner approval. Species/local accession and cultivar caveats remain intact. Common yarrow retains its uncertain native/nursery lineage classification. No new external botanical facts were researched for this read-only-source pass.

Healthy/flowering and dry/spent variants are mutually exclusive choices for one placed instance. Do not spawn every variant in a group's state list. Different placed copies can choose different states. The four alternative IDs are `plant.san_diego_monkeyflower::dry_sparse`, `plant.san_diego_sunflower::dry_spent`, `plant.coyote_brush::mild_dry_stress`, and `plant.common_yarrow::spent_flower`. They were generated independently; align each variant with its own anchor/scale rather than treating them as registered animation frames.

## Holds and corrections

The older `plant.purple_needlegrass::healthy` remains on morphology HOLD. `healthy.seed_morphology_r01` is the explicit improved provisional option, with expert/style/owner and fine-awn small-display review still pending. The original flattened healthy sycamore and Torrey pine are whole-tree options; their separately generated wood/canopy layers remain components with provisional registration.

Engelmann oak wood/canopy remain on the later attachment HOLD. The attachment-method foliage materials are research only and are omitted from production entries. Neither the old full canopy nor the experimental leaf clusters become accepted starter options here. Detached Engelmann leaf/acorn materials remain catalogued as accessories, not a resolved tree.

Two inherited metadata issues are preserved with explicit audit errata. Sycamore's old wind schema incorrectly says `needle_tips`; `effective_starter_wind_metadata` replaces that group with broadleaf leaf clusters. Several original rights records still say “Pending generation”; actual generated bytes and generation states prevail. The original wording is retained, and applicable image-generation-service terms still govern. No actual wind rig, motion, animation, physics or leaf emitter is implemented by these metadata corrections.

## Contact sheets and intake

Light/dark whole-plant and dry/spent contact sheets use one shared 32 px/m illustrative scale, nearest-neighbor sampling, and source placement anchors. They are compact display-scale checks, not actual application screenshots or calibrated runtime approval. The two trees occupy much more space than low plants; low plant detail is intentionally small. Main sheets show one primary option per family, with the improved needlegrass revision replacing the held older candidate. Ground lines and labels are contact annotations only. They are not sprite pixels.

Independent plant sprites remain editable placeables. Vegetation already painted into yard backgrounds remains a different baked layer. This audit does not extract or promise separation of baked vegetation.

The catalog is ready for coordinated intake as a compatibility view. Source scoped reviews remain preserved; Mac integration, saved-yard placement, owner acceptance and runtime testing are pending. No app, Git, shared root index or intake changes occur in this pass. Reproduce locally with `python build_catalog.py` while the referenced original yard-production source tree is available. Source manifest snapshots allow review without that tree, but contact regeneration requires the existing sprite bytes.
