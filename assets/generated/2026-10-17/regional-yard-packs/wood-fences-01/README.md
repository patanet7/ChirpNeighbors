# San Diego wooden fences, four seasons

Three matching fence families supply12 independent RGBA components: section, closed gate, corner and post in cedar privacy, weathered vertical boards and light aged picket. Four actual seasonal coastal foundations combine with three independent fence-row layers to make12 reproducible yard scenes. The foreground has no baked movable plants, pots or furniture.

[View the actual12-scene contact sheet](files/expansion/fences-01/root/ChirpNeighbors-Wood-Fences-Four-Seasons.png). Use [fence-catalog.json](fence-catalog.json) for repository-root sprite paths, source hashes, anchors, footprints, endpoints, seasonal keys, interaction proposals and scene layers. [manifest.json](manifest.json) reconciles exact image/prompt bytes and public metadata. Run `python3 assets/generated/2026-10-17/regional-yard-packs/wood-fences-01/verify_fences.py` from the repository root.

Each foundation is flattened sky/distance/ground art. Fence rows and individual components are truly separate alpha images. Composited review PNGs are flattened previews. All spring/summer/autumn/winter keys are supplied, with mild coastal winter and no default snow. Fence materials remain static across seasons.

Generation did not preserve a common physical pixel scale or exact2m/1m spans. Review rows use uniform per-component nearest-neighbor scaling. Front-facing joins were reviewed; corner projection and seamless snapping remain provisional. Closed leaves have proposed hinges/latches/clearance, flat caps have potential perch anchors, and all wood is rigid with wind weight0. Metadata does not implement behavior, gate motion, wind or physics. Minor alpha residue, seasonal strip drift and missing independent contact shadows are retained in review notes.

Source-only candidates are generated, scoped-reviewed and staged. Runtime integration, automatic season selection and owner acceptance remain false. No Mac application or active runtime catalog files changed. Exact prompts/source originals and small immutable transport bundles were delivered separately; private Library/conversation locators and ZIP wrappers are excluded from Git.
