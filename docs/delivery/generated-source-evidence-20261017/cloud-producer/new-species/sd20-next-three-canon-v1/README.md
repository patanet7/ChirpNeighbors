# Lesser Goldfinch, Northern Mockingbird and California Scrub-Jay starter canons

One actual native OpenAI image-generation call produced **six new drawings**, two poses of each of three missing species from the verified San Diego roster. This packet proposes **three source idle/look loops**; it does not claim any actor, locomotion or feeding implementation. `original.png` retains the untouched 1536×1024 RGBA output. Exact instructions and original hash are in `prompt.txt` and `receipts/generation.json`. The existing House Finch drawing was inspected and supplied only as the illustration-style reference. No Blue Jay artwork was relabelled.

The drawings show a western green-backed adult male Lesser Goldfinch, a plain-breasted adult Northern Mockingbird, and a crestless adult California Scrub-Jay with gray-brown back, white eyebrow and partial blue necklace. Primary morphology and length references were checked 2026-10-17: [Cornell Lesser Goldfinch](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Lesser_Goldfinch/id), [Cornell Northern Mockingbird](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Northern_Mockingbird/id), [Cornell California Scrub-Jay](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/California_Scrub-Jay/id). Biological reported lengths are 9–11cm, 21–26cm and 28–30cm. Factual descriptions informed the prompt; no reference-photo pixels were downloaded or incorporated. Regional resident support remains the parent SDNHM-backed roster; no abundance or home-sighting claim is made.

Every source frame uses a 1024×1024 canvas and registration root [608,800]. Principal alpha≥32 components assign native pixels with alpha≥2 to each individual; alpha1 residue is excluded from derivatives only. Integer translation aligns a coarse foot-ground root. No source repainting, per-pose fitting, scale correction, head rotation or rig synthesis was used. Original source bytes remain untouched. Source frame IDs hash the exact registered RGBA pixels, not compressed PNG bytes; both are recorded.

The small goldfinch exports at 96×96. The larger mockingbird and jay export at **192×192** to preserve detail around normal yard size. Both poses of an individual share one canvas, one output scale and one presentation density. Neutral visible lengths are proposed at **36, 80 and 98 world pixels**, respectively. The look-pose visible widths are approximately 34.94, 75.10 and 89.66 world pixels: head angle and drawing differences alter bounding boxes, with no per-pose rescaling. Biological length is an external species reference; these widths are practical bounding-box presentation proxies, not calibrated anatomical bill-to-tail measurements. Final world scale remains provisional.

`review.html` plays the actual RGBA APNG files at proposed world scale: neutral 60 ticks, look 6 ticks, neutral 24 ticks, at 30Hz. Total 3 seconds, look hold 200ms. Separate neutral/look PNGs, APNG and GIF previews and per-species review manifests are supplied. `normal-size-preview.png` is an art contact sheet, not an application screenshot. Its upper row shows normal world size; lower diagnostic crops use 2× goldfinch output pixels and 1× larger-species output pixels. The HTML can enlarge each to 4× its output pixels.
