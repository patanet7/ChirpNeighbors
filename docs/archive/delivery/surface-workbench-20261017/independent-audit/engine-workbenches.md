## Findings that affect the delivery claim

1. **P1 — the latest-art garden does not establish integration into the saved yard.** `/` normally passes saved birds, assets and objects into `YardScene`, but passes no physical-surface, water, weather-surface, directional-wind or seasonal-tree options. The development-only “latest garden” button replaces that component with `GardenDemoExperience`, the same component used by `/garden-demo`. Its visual renderer is a separate SVG/image composite; its runtime hardcodes one jay and one bath, while cardinal/dove art is held separately. Some real simulator/contact/fountain primitives are shared, so this is useful integration work, but it is not the saved yard's actor population, layout, asset pins or Pixi scene. ## Three corrective slices and acceptance
